About this ministry

Who we are

Apologetics Faith exists to help Christians think clearly and help seekers ask honestly.

Our mission

We believe that Christian faith is not blind — it is grounded in history, evidence, and reason. Apologetics Faith is a ministry dedicated to equipping believers to defend their faith and to helping honest seekers find answers to their hardest questions.

Why apologetics?

The word "apologetics" comes from the Greek apologia — a defence or reasoned argument. The apostle Peter commanded every believer to be ready to give a reason for the hope within them (1 Peter 3:15). This is not a task for scholars alone. Every Christian can and should be able to explain why they believe what they believe.

What we cover

Our articles address the most common and most difficult questions about Christianity — the resurrection of Jesus, the reliability of the Bible, the existence of God, the problem of suffering, the relationship between science and faith, and comparisons with other worldviews.

About the author

Apologetics Faith is written and maintained by Anthony William, based in Tanzania. Anthony is passionate about making Christian apologetics accessible to Swahili-speaking and East African audiences, and to anyone wrestling with questions of faith.
